# Artes do carrossel

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens, a pedido do usuário.
São composições ilustrativas de possibilidades de personalização; não capturas
exatas nem novos temas instalados. Os arquivos usados no site ficam em
`solid-site/public/brand/templates/*-art.webp` e na cópia de `apps/web/public`.
As capturas `*.jpg` registram os modelos reais usados na primeira versão.

## Prompts finais

## essencial

Use case: ui-mockup / ads-marketing. Create ONE polished landscape 3:2 promotional artwork for a premium Brazilian checkout template carousel. Theme: ESSENCIAL. Subject: luxury everyday apparel, folded cream sweatshirt with a beautiful small product photograph. Palette: warm sand, ivory and charcoal black. Art direction: professional software product launch artwork, beautifully prepared not a raw wireframe or empty form screenshot. A large floating desktop checkout UI panel fills the left 72% and a tall mobile checkout UI panel overlaps its right edge, both almost front-on for readability, premium crisp typography, carefully aligned grids, soft studio shadows. Cohesive softly lit textured gradient backdrop with broad abstract ribbons and restrained depth, matching this theme. Both UI panels fully inside the artwork with safe margins. Actual supported structure: two-column checkout: wide identification form on the left, order summary on right. Brand header 'ESSENCIAL', product thumbnail, 'Identificação', 'Entrega', 'Pagamento' progress, 'Nome completo', 'E-mail', 'Celular', 'Resumo do pedido', price 'R$ 149,00' and main action 'Continuar'. Show the relevant attractive product photography IN the checkout product thumbnail and a tasteful branded banner, enough realistic visual content so it feels ready to sell. The mobile panel must be a responsive rendering of same brand and product. All text in Portuguese. No large headline outside the UI, no unrelated logos, no fake customer reviews, no sales statistics, no invented certifications, no credit card or wallet methods, no scannable QR code, no confidential data. Premium realistic web design within editable checkout capabilities (brand, banner, colors, fields, summary), not a futuristic dashboard. High quality raster image, landscape 1536x1024.
